**Australian Superannuation : Technology Risk in Change & Digital Control Governance**

**Overview**
Governance of technology change across a regulated superannuation digital environment covering the member mobile application, public website, secure member portal, MFA, platform upgrades and production releases.

**GRC Activities & Controls**
- Coordinated risk and control-readiness/effectiveness assessments before production deployment.
- Supported Security Risk Reviews, Architecture Risk Reviews and Solution Risk Assessments for material digital changes.
- Maintained visibility of risk ownership, treatment actions, control gaps, dependencies and inherent/residual risk considerations.
- Escalated residual risk and supported risk acceptance/escalation decisions where implementation or control conditions were not fully met.
- Managed UAT/PVT evidence, dependencies, defects and unresolved issues.
- Supported CAB/change approval and production-governance processes.
- Coordinated security, architecture, platform, product and vendor dependencies.
- Tracked risks, issues, remediation actions and production validation.
- Supported implementation of MFA as an identity and access security control.
- Escalated release blockers and residual concerns for management decisions.

**Outcome / Risk Reduction**

Supported controlled delivery of 12 major releases in six months and implementation of MFA for 1M+ members, strengthening change control, access security and production-readiness practices.
